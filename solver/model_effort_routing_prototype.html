Model, effort and shared-capacity routing

Question: which role/model/effort cells may a deterministic Inference governor admit, and what may it change when capacity, time, progress or evidence becomes uncertain?

Evidence boundary. One fresh micro-fixture per cell, 28 sequential calls, host-native pinned Codex CLI, no web. The production local Board and container harness were unavailable; effective Daybreak snapshot was unreported. The generic weekly window moved 3%→4% during Daybreak and stayed at 4% through Luna. This screens candidates; it does not Gate-approve them or set numeric burn shares.

Drive the governor

The pure decision function never treats unknown as spare capacity, never changes route on shared exhaustion, and never escalates from model-authored uncertainty alone.

Burn envelope policy

Keep numeric shares unset. One trial per cell and a whole-percent account window cannot estimate role weights or a five-hour allowance.

Reserve is symbolic until matched repeated Runs measure it: one admitted Recovery Turn at its P95 usage and wall time, plus the fixed Run tail. A new discretionary Turn starts only when both remain provably available.

Once a Turn starts, its role/model/effort freezes. A Checkpoint continues the same cell; a Claim of uncertainty changes nothing. Shared exhaustion enters Quota wait. It never selects Luna, CPA or another effort as fictional capacity.

Unknown-state behavior

Unknown catalog, effective model, applicable bucket, reset outcome or reserve blocks a new discretionary Turn. Work already in flight finishes inside its bound. Deterministic Intake, preparation and Recovery observation continue.

A reserved Recovery invocation may start only from a preflight-frozen, still-provable envelope. Otherwise the Run records why it cannot prove authority and waits; zero is never inferred.

Time pressure narrows starts by measured completion envelopes. It does not downgrade quality or turn one micro-fixture into a numeric threshold.
