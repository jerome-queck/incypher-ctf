During the competition, each team is prohibited from:
- Collaborating with anyone outside the team in any form (flag sharing, cross teaming, etc).
- Discuss/ask about challenges in the Discord server. If you have a question, please open a ticket.
- Using LLMs or other automated methods to create multiple accounts for the purpose of submitting flags through those accounts.
- Using automated scanners or tools such as SQLMap, Burp Scanner, Dirb, or similar tools (unless explicitly permitted in the challenge description).
- Causing any form of harm or disruption to other participants or the organizers. Examples include:
- Deleting or modifying flag files,
- Launching DDoS attacks against services or the competition platform,
- Brute-forcing flags on the platform,
- And other similar disruptive actions.
- Engaging in flag hoarding (intentionally stockpiling flags and submitting them in bulk near the end of the competition).
- Hints will be given only to challenges which have 0 solves. The organizer will notify when a hint is released, so please do not ask for hints in the Discord server, or anywhere else.
AI Policy
Human Division
- No AI assistance is allowed besides browser AI overview and IDE code suggestions and autocomplete.
- Even though AI overview is allowed, you are not allowed to prompt it. Only use it to help summarize the browser results.
AI Assistant Division
Use any AI/LLM you want.
- If you want to switch from the Human Division to the AI-Assisted Division, open a ticket on our Discord server and we will review your case. Switching from the AI-Assisted Division to the Human Division is not allowed.
Teams found to have violated these rules or engaged in cheating will be subject to penalties, including disqualification or point deductions.
Flag format for each challenge will be COMPFEST18{[A-z0-9_-]+} unless specified otherwise.
