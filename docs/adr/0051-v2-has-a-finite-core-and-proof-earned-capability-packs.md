# v2 has a finite Core and proof-earned Capability Packs

[Bound v2 to a fieldable Core and earned Capability
Packs](https://github.com/jerome-queck/incypher-ctf/issues/242) replaces the unbounded rule that
every pre-release-specifiable capability is mandatory. **v2 has one finite mandatory Core; exactly
three predeclared Capability Packs may join a candidate only after their own proof.** A missed
coordination target never stops useful v2, v3, evidence or venue work. The only terminal calendar
constraint is a fieldable candidate before the scored competition starts on 22 September morning.

This supersedes ADR-0033's unlimited v2 scope and sequential-calendar clauses; ADR-0039 and the
native-primary clauses carried through ADR-0014, ADR-0032, ADR-0040 and ADR-0042; ADR-0046's
mandatory model-assisted novel Recovery and practice image-repair scope; ADR-0048's mandatory
automatic-training scope; ADR-0049 only where it requires a Capability Pack or optional supporting
infrastructure to pass v2; and ADR-0050 only where it makes the full Observer mandatory. Their
remaining safety, authority, Harness, evidence and interface decisions stand.

## Core is finite, supported and proved

The **v2 Core** is the smallest safe, fieldable pre-final capability set. Every release candidate
that claims a v2 pass must implement it end to end, expose no placeholder path and carry its
required controlled and Gate evidence:

- Intake; deterministic Triage and Order baselines; model-assisted Triage and adaptive routing;
  one persistent Solve Lead; support for two Lanes and mediated Specialists; Target, Lease,
  bounded submission and reserved-tail lifecycles.
- Both native Codex and the private single-owner CPA route. One sealed qualification result selects
  the release-candidate profile's primary route from hardware, compatibility, policy and matched
  evidence;
  CPA is not inherently a fallback. Both routes obey the same Attempt, Tool, state, deadline,
  isolation and accounting contracts and share one subscription, backend and quota. A classified
  primary-route-local fault may use the other proved route; shared exhaustion never causes a route
  change.
- Brokered credentials and typed egress; proved Attempt isolation and resource limits; a minimal
  Supervisor; canonical effects, replay and restart; deterministic containment; and a safe
  no-inference posture.
- The required fieldable Tool profiles; minimal diagnostic and Solve receipts; sanitized Run
  capsules; mechanism, safety and conformance tests; controlled proofs; and the full-window Gate.

Core means **implemented and proved support**, not permanent activation. The exact candidate
profile may enable one or two Lanes, zero to two Specialist engagements and the evidence-selected
model, effort, route and Tool configuration. Disabled Core capacity remains available in the same
image and has its own proof; configuration cannot erase its safety contract.

Official evidence may invalidate any pre-release premise, including the route, packaging,
isolation or hardware model. That produces a cited v3 supersession, not a dishonest v2 pass or an
unexplained Core omission.

## Only three Capability Packs may earn admission

A **Capability Pack** is a coherent optional bundle which supplies no Core safety invariant, can be
disabled independently and is not a Version. The closed v2 set is:

1. model-assisted diagnosis and remedy selection for a novel Recovery Incident, beyond Core's
   deterministic detection, containment, restart, no-inference safety and fixed remedies;
2. the full host-side Observer CLI, beyond Core's canonical records and minimal sanitized
   diagnostic receipts; and
3. the external practice candidate-image repair controller, including diagnose, patch, build,
   test, probation, promotion and rollback.

The third Pack never runs inside the Solver, never enters a scored image and never gives the Solver
a dependency on the practice rig or the actual IN-CYPHER backend implementation. The competition
rig, Evaluator, Targets, sealed holdouts,
protocol/fault suites and full-window orchestration are external evidence infrastructure: the
Solver sees only ordinary Board, Target and inference surfaces and remains independently
deployable against any implementation that satisfies those contracts. Synthetic populations,
automatic datasets, fitting or model promotion, elaborate
repair orchestration and broad cross-architecture semantic parity are optional external supporting
work, neither Solver Core nor Capability Packs.

Each Pack enters an exact release candidate only when it is implemented, independently disableable
and passes its declared proof. Otherwise it is disabled or omitted without failing Core. Omission
keeps the source issue and reason; it never silently becomes v3.

## One manifest tells the truth about one release candidate

Every exact image and configuration seals a **Release-candidate manifest**. Each planned capability
is classified `core` or `pack` and records implementation, proof, evidence, enablement or omission,
source issue and reason. A Core row without its required proof keeps v2 incomplete. A Pack row
without proof stays disabled or omitted. The manifest also identifies the primary inference route
and the exact Lane, Specialist, model, effort and Tool configuration selected for that candidate.

A safest proved partial release candidate may still reach the final go/no-go. Fielding it does not turn an
incomplete v2 into a pass, relabel known work as v3 or conceal an omission.

## Targets coordinate; they do not stop progress

The operating targets are:

- before the first official capture on 14 September, produce a buildable candidate whose Core
  paths exist end to end and whose manifest declares every proof or gap;
- from 14 September, adapt to cited official rules, ADK, PoW, Board, Target, auth, submission and
  hardware facts as v3 while late generic Core work continues as v2;
- by 16 September 18:00 SGT, prefer to admit only Packs already implemented, disableable and proved;
- by 18 September 18:00 SGT, prefer to select the exact digest and configuration for the
  full-window Gate;
- on 19 September, run the 10:30–16:00 SGT full-window Gate; use 20 September for a critical
  correction and replacement evidence if needed; and
- on 21 September, rehearse at the venue, capture the real environment and hardware, apply only
  necessary cited v3 changes, rerun the affected proof and then freeze the fielded candidate.

These are coordination and evidence targets, not workflow locks. Missing one makes status late,
incomplete or unproved and must be recorded, but v2 implementation, v3 adaptation, Pack proof,
testing and venue work continue wherever they remain useful. Generic facts and defects remain v2
even after 14 September; only a cited official artifact, observation or ruling can trigger v3.
The final hard deadline is the start of the scored competition on 22 September morning.

## Consequences

- Scope pressure becomes visible in one manifest instead of being hidden by an impossible
  all-or-nothing roadmap.
- The two inference routes, productive topology, adaptive model policy and fieldable Tools remain
  genuine Core obligations even when the selected scored profile keeps some capacity disabled.
- Optional model Recovery, Observer convenience and practice image repair cannot delay a safe
  fieldable candidate.
- v2 and v3 may progress concurrently without laundering generic work into the official delta.
- Passing v2 still requires its Core evidence; dates do not manufacture a pass, and a failed Gate
  does not forbid subsequent work or the final go/no-go.

## Revisit when

- Official rules, hardware or interfaces invalidate a Core premise.
- Controlled or full-window evidence shows the finite Core is not safely fieldable.
- The 21 September venue rehearsal exposes a necessary competition-fit change.
- A proposed fourth Capability Pack appears; it requires an explicit decision rather than quiet
  scope growth.
