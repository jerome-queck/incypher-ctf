# One score basis qualifies one release-candidate profile

> **Selection campaign superseded by [ADR-0059](0059-v2-is-the-final-competition-candidate.md).** A
> six-arm campaign is not required before fielding; choose one working configuration through
> bounded smoke/rehearsal evidence and spend the remaining time on official compatibility.

[What objective and evidence qualify the selected v2
policy?](https://github.com/jerome-queck/incypher-ctf/issues/243) resolves the contradiction between
ADR-0043's inferential promotion rule and ADR-0049's descriptive three-pair rule. **The Solver
optimises final official Board score inside non-negotiable legality, autonomy, authority and safety
constraints; score availability never controls Run liveness; and one exact Release-candidate
profile qualifies through three predeclared descriptive matched pairs before its full-window Gate.**

This record replaces ADR-0035's Flags-first objective; ADR-0042, ADR-0043 and ADR-0048's matched
5.5-hour component matrices; ADR-0043's one-sided 95-percent promotion bounds; ADR-0049's
Flags-first comparison order; and ADR-0049's requirement that the full-window Run exercise two
Lanes and a Specialist even when the selected profile enables less. Their deterministic control,
zero-tolerance safety, resource envelopes, support obligations, one full-window Gate and honest
claim boundaries stand.

## Score is the objective, never a liveness dependency

The competition objective is the final official **Board score** held by the team at official close.
First place is the destination, but local evidence cannot measure placement without the official
scoring rule and opposing population. A policy never trades a disqualification, Intervention,
unauthorised effect, lost authority fact, isolation breach, hard-envelope breach or early terminal
Run for score. Within those constraints score leads; speed follows; resource and subscription
efficiency are reported and may break no performance loss.

One **Score basis** is sealed before a matched pair or Run:

1. `official` applies only when a published scoring formula and every authoritative input needed to
   reproduce it are available;
2. `scenario-weighted` applies the hidden Evaluator's predeclared population schedule and closing
   Challenge values as a local proxy, never as an official-performance claim; or
3. `unit` assigns one to every Gate-qualified solve.

The first applicable basis wins before the pair or Run begins. An unavailable, partial, stale or
unsettled score is never zero. Before that boundary, bounded Recovery may try to settle its source,
then the ladder falls through to `unit` where necessary. Once a pair's first arm or a Run starts,
the basis cannot change; later evidence may change only a later pair or Run. If a sealed non-unit
result cannot be reconstructed, that arm finishes but its pair is inconclusive unless a predeclared
shared defect invalidates the pair.

Runtime Order has the same non-blocking boundary without confusing evaluation with scheduling. A
complete trustworthy Challenge-value snapshot may supply Order's payoff component. If any eligible
Challenge's value in that coherent snapshot is absent or unsettled, the whole snapshot uses unit
value for that component; other Order evidence continues to discriminate. A later settled snapshot
is read only at the next Attempt boundary. Board score, Challenge value and Order value are three
different facts; no intermediate Challenge value is called points banked at acceptance because CTFd
may recompute it retroactively.

## One exact profile is selected; every supported boundary is still proved

The Release-candidate manifest seals one **Release-candidate profile** whose digest binds:

- primary and alternate Inference routes and the classified route-change policy;
- enabled Lane and Specialist topology;
- requested and effective model identities, effort, prompt bundle and Tool policy;
- Triage, Order, Tier, Cut, submission and Resource dials; and
- every enabled in-image Capability Pack which can change scored behaviour.

Host-only Observer and practice-repair Packs retain their own admission proof and stay outside the
scored-performance comparison unless they consume scored-Run resources or change Solver behaviour.
The first incumbent uses the same complete Core image, Tools, authority, isolation, alternate-route
support and safety envelopes as its challengers, with one Lane, no Specialists, deterministic
Triage, one persistent Solve Lead and no behaviour-changing Packs. Its route, model and effort are
the simplest component-calibration cell which did not lose qualified Flags.

Core support is not activation. Every supported combination, including the maximum two-Lane,
two-Specialist configuration, passes deterministic mechanism, adversarial concurrency, authority,
isolation and Resource-envelope proofs. That maximum is a safety-stress configuration even when it
is not selected. Performance qualification and the full-window Gate use the exact selected profile;
a selected one-Lane, zero-Specialist profile is never forced to masquerade as two Lanes and a
Specialist merely to satisfy a safety-support row.

## Calibration, selection and the Gate never share a family

Evidence advances through four distinct stages:

1. deterministic mechanisms and the complete supported-configuration safety matrix;
2. component calibration over calibration corpus;
3. three matched whole-profile pairs over fresh Selection holdouts; and
4. one 5.5-hour full-window Run over sixteen untouched Discovery holdouts.

Challenge family and generator lineage are disjoint across the three corpus roles. Component
screens may use calibration evidence. Each Selection-holdout family is shared only by the two arms
of one matched pair. Result exposure or any influence on a model, prompt, Tool, route, topology,
Tier, Order weight or other dial retires its whole family permanently to calibration or regression;
resetting, reflagging, renaming or rejecting the result does not restore freshness. The full-window
sixteen remain untouched until the Release-candidate profile is already selected.

Candidate-owned failure, missing output, Cut, refusal, exhaustion or early terminality is an
observed loss, not an exclusion. All failed and inconclusive arms and Runs remain retained. Only a
predeclared rig, Evaluator or shared-service defect which destroys causal comparability invalidates
the whole pair and permits a fresh replacement. A score read disappearing is non-terminal; where
the sealed result cannot be computed it makes the pair inconclusive rather than erasing either arm.

## Three bounded pairs select the whole profile

Before any Selection holdout is exposed, component calibration applies a predeclared nomination
rule to a finite candidate set and freezes exactly one complete challenger profile. One campaign's
three fresh pairs compare only that challenger against the lean incumbent and select the winner.
The result cannot nominate, modify or re-enter a profile. A remaining or materially changed profile
is a new candidate for a later separately sealed campaign over fresh Selection families. This
process selects between its two nominated profiles without claiming a global optimum.

Each arm starts at sixty minutes with six Challenges in two release waves. A pilot using calibration
families may extend every future arm once to ninety minutes if the lean incumbent cannot produce two
Gate-qualified solves or exercise its enabled topology. The duration then freezes before any
Selection holdout is exposed; a result never earns an extension.

The two arms of each pair freeze identical Challenge bytes, release and population schedules,
faults, Score basis, Resource limits, model catalogue and external conditions. Board and Solver
state, identities, Instances and inaccessible caches reset between arms, and no prior-arm artifact
is reachable. The order is counterbalanced `incumbent/challenger`, `challenger/incumbent`, then
`incumbent/challenger`. Starting host, quota and route state must lie inside their predeclared bands.
The Scenario manifests, invalidation rules, analysis code and decision rule seal before unblinding.

The primary `basis_value` is the final official formula applied to the complete authoritative
outcome under `official`, the sum of closing Scenario values for Gate-qualified solves under
`scenario-weighted`, and `q` under `unit`. Official comparison therefore includes any published
bonus, penalty or non-additive term rather than silently reducing Board score to Challenge values.

For a horizon `T`, total closing Scenario value `V`, accepted-value function `a(t)`, qualified-solve
count `q` and released-Challenge count `n`, the receipt also computes:

```text
weighted_share  = sum(closing value of Gate-qualified solves) / V
unit_share      = q / n
score_time_area = (1 / (T * V)) * integral from 0 to T of a(t) dt
```

Under the `unit` basis each value is one and `V = n`. `a(t)` uses accepted-verdict time and the
eventual closing value, never Candidate discovery time or a fictional value banked when the Flag was
submitted. Unsolved, unattempted, Retrieval-contaminated and Unqualified Challenges remain
zero-valued members of the denominator. These common additive metrics never replace a non-additive
official formula. v3 must predeclare a formula-faithful official score-time tie metric before its
pair starts; without one, an all-tied official comparison is inconclusive and retains the incumbent.

All six arms must be safety-clean. A challenger qualifies only when its final Score-basis value wins
at least two of three paired deltas and the sum of those deltas is positive. Before official scoring,
the sum of its unit-share deltas must also be nonnegative. If every final-score delta is exactly
zero, the same two-of-three and positive-sum rule applies to score-time area under the proxy bases.
The unit-share guard applies only while Scenario weights are a synthetic proxy: an invented local
weight may not buy fewer qualified solves and call that official-score progress. Mixed, missing or
otherwise inconclusive evidence retains the incumbent. Tokens, Turns, Board requests, CPU, RAM,
PIDs, disk, network, coordination, instrumentation and other measured tails remain reported context;
inside proved envelopes they never compensate for lost score.

These are descriptive admission rules, not statistical inference. Three pairs cannot support
ADR-0043's one-sided 95-percent non-inferiority or superiority bounds. The receipt reports every
arm, paired delta, sum, minimum, median and maximum without a significance claim. No axis-wise win
is assumed to compose: component trials nominate profiles, while only a fresh whole-profile result
may select one.

## The full-window Gate proves the selected profile, not superiority

The one 5.5-hour Gate Run uses the immutable selected profile and ADR-0049's sixteen family-disjoint
Discovery holdouts. Its existing absolute `5 / 16`, three-Category, final-90-minute release,
full-window, alternate-route, final-submission, isolation, authority, resource and zero-tolerance
safety floors stand. Weighted share, unit share and score-time area are reported, but no arbitrary
local weight becomes an additional pass threshold. The full Run is integration, endurance and
capability proof; the pairs selected the profile, and the controlled suites proved disabled Core
capacity and the maximal safety configuration.

A local pass supports exact-candidate mechanism and safety claims, causal claims confined to the
controlled matched comparison, and descriptive performance on the sealed local corpus. It does not
support a claim about official score, rank, population performance, statistical superiority,
production load, organiser ADK, PoW, Target, deployment compatibility or human strategy. The
`solved / 16` Clopper-Pearson interval remains descriptive rather than turning one selected corpus
into a competition-wide estimate.

## Official scoring is a cited v3 input

When organisers publish scoring, v3 seals the official formula and authoritative inputs. Existing
qualified outcomes may be replayed under it when their receipts carry every required fact. If the
formula changes the selected profile, or needs a fact the receipts did not record, fresh v3
whole-profile pairs decide the affected choice. The full-window Gate repeats only when that process
materially changes the release candidate. Missing official score still cannot halt a Run; `unit`
remains the graceful fallback and the manifest says which basis was used.

## One receipt preserves the selection

One immutable machine-readable **Policy-qualification receipt** binds:

- incumbent and challenger Release-candidate profile and image digests;
- Score basis, source provenance, fallback and missingness;
- all six arm and Scenario receipts, arm order, analysis digest and predeclared rules;
- starting host, quota, model-catalogue and route observations;
- validity or invalidation reasons, raw metrics, paired deltas and admission verdict; and
- every Selection-holdout family and its retirement record.

The Release-candidate manifest links the receipt selected for its profile. A change to the Solver
image, any profile field above, effective model identity, or safety or Resource envelope creates a
materially new candidate and requires fresh whole-profile pairs and a new full-window Gate. A
Scenario or analysis change invalidates only the evidence which used it. Documentation and
receipt-only corrections change no candidate. Official-score replay follows the v3 rule above.

## Consequences and handoff

- Score or no score, every otherwise healthy Run keeps working and states its basis honestly.
- The selected profile may be simpler than the maximum configuration without erasing Core support.
- A bounded descriptive rule replaces impossible precision while retaining every failure and the
  lower incumbent on uncertainty.
- Production telemetry, Order-value naming, manifests, receipts, rig scenarios and controller logic
  follow through `/to-spec` and `/to-tickets`; this decision implements none of them in v1.
- Wayfinder map 153 remains open for a separate full-map coherence audit before `/to-spec`.
