# One passing full-window Run closes v2 after controlled proofs

> **The Gate scope is amended by
> [ADR-0051](0051-v2-has-a-finite-core-and-proof-earned-capability-packs.md).** Core rows remain
> mandatory; a Capability Pack contributes rows only when the exact Release-candidate manifest admits it.
> Optional external training, population and repair machinery cannot fail v2 merely by omission.

[What evidence makes v2 ready to
freeze](https://github.com/jerome-queck/incypher-ctf/issues/196) fixes the evidence contract left
open by ADR-0033 and ADR-0048. **v2 passes only after exhaustive deterministic and accelerated
proofs pass and one immutable release candidate completes one passing 5.5-hour Run on the local
competition rig.** Requiring three full Runs would spend time v2 does not have without making a
three-Run sample statistically persuasive; treating one Run as statistical proof would be equally
false. The one Run is an integration, endurance and capability proof, while the controlled suites
carry the exhaustive and causal claims.

## One ledger reaches one verdict

The Gate evaluates ADR-0048's machine-readable requirement ledger clause by clause. Each row names
its source decision, venue, scenario or fixture, exact query, expected observation, metric,
pass/fail rule and receipt. A row passes only from its named evidence. A green process exit, prose
claim or aggregate score cannot substitute for a missing row, and no success can average away a
safety failure.

The Gate verdict is:

- **pass** only when every locally testable v2 row passes and the full-window Run passes;
- **fail** when a testable row fails, required evidence is missing, or the release candidate does
  not complete its required proof; or
- **Pending** only for official compatibility and v1's real-Instance clause, which no pre-release
  local binding can prove and which expire at v3's final go/no-go under ADR-0026 and ADR-0033.

The local CTFd plus chall-manager competition rig is the mandatory and only controllable full-Board
v2 Gate venue. Historical promoted Brunner Runs remain regression and real-Board context, not fresh
release-candidate proof. A later suitable external Board may add evidence but is never a v2
prerequisite. The local rig does not prove the organiser's ADK, PoW, Target, production load, human
population or deployment compatibility.

## Controlled proof precedes the full Run

Before the 5.5-hour Run starts, the exact candidate image and policy must pass all applicable
deterministic mechanism suites, accelerated scenarios, adversarial isolation tests, fault matrices
and semantic Tool-capability probes. These proofs cover at least:

- Intake refresh, releases and changed Artefacts; malformed, stale, conflicting and unsettled Board
  reads; Crowd qualification; Triage replay; Order and Tier recomputation;
- accepted, rejected, duplicate, delayed and indeterminate submissions; Instance deployment,
  trustworthy reads, renewal, expiry, termination, Mana refusal and reserved-tail cleanup;
- Attempt acquisition and closing barriers, named Cuts, carry and Work-generation disposition,
  Checkpoint epochs, late output, damaged successor segments, Triage-batch idempotent reuse, state
  pressure, reachability cleanup, Promotion, reconstruction and trend recomposition;
- parallel-safe Challenge claims, two-Lane Lease ownership, epoch fencing, startup reconciliation,
  stale effects, natural expiry, earlier-Run residue and unknown Instance attribution;
- process ownership, isolation, resource pressure, hostile content, long legitimate work, hangs,
  runaway output, escaped children and stray-process removal;
- Recovery containment at owner-local, Lane and run-shared scope; inference faults, shared
  exhaustion, Quota wait and every pre-authorised reset outcome; and
- model, effort, prompt, Tool, Harness, topology, Specialist, Triage Judge and Observer evidence
  required by their source decisions, including requested, catalogued and effective model/effort
  identity; shared-limit movement; refusals; Recovery-reserve impact; tail pressure; quota
  uncertainty and exhaustion; identity drift; and interrupted Turns. Hard Specialist progression
  must advance Daybreak only `high -> xhigh -> max`, only between Turns and only one step after a
  replay-confirmed Checkpoint. Astra is screened only on an authorised hard/stretch Specialist at
  high or max: one platform rejection excludes it immediately, one model refusal receives exactly
  one independent confirmation, and a second refusal excludes it without persuasion retries.

Every required Tool capability passes its real-input semantic probe on the native arm64 image.
The amd64 image must build and pass its smoke and built-image probes; emulation is not required for
another 5.5-hour Run. Package presence, import success and `command -v` are not capability proof.

Every safety boundary has zero tolerance. The suites admit no duplicate or unauthorised submission,
wrapper brute force, stale-epoch effect, overlapping Lease binding, cross-Lane termination, unsafe
adoption or destruction, falsely closed Instance, unowned process, public DNS or internet access
from an Attempt executor beyond its selected inference route and Target, oracle or answer-bearing
access, sibling-work access, OOM, PID or disk exhaustion, control/Recovery-reserve breach, or
unresolved secret in Promotion. The isolation query must positively prove that arbitrary public DNS,
HTTP and raw TCP fail. Search-enabled rehearsals are non-qualifying and never enter the Gate
denominator. An indeterminate submission is reconciled before retry; every accepted Flag has a
complete Solve receipt. A successful isolation probe fails the Gate, and an inconclusive breach
whose scope cannot be bounded invalidates its Run.

Legitimate long cryptography, compilation, browser, emulation and forensics fixtures declare their
owner, deadline, envelope and progress probe before launch. Each must remain alive through its
expected Checkpoint or completion while a matched silent hang or runaway is contained. Killing the
legitimate fixture merely for age, heartbeat cadence or temporary idleness fails the applicable
Recovery row.

Recovery retains ADR-0046's upper deadlines: a hard fault is owned and contained within 15 seconds,
unexplained inactivity seals a diagnostic snapshot within 60 seconds, the condition is rechecked by
120 seconds, and an unresolved condition has a Recovery Agent active by 180 seconds. Healthy work
continues unless shared authority or durable effect recording is ambiguous.

Canonical replay over the sealed stream must reproduce identical Run, Boot, Lane, Attempt, Lease,
submission and Order-input projections and durable Triage judgements without another model call or
external effect. A replacement Boot closes interrupted Attempts as `crashed`, quarantines late
output, preserves only validated bounded carry, reconstructs authority and uses fresh identities.
Corrupt or missing authority fails closed. Every Attempt ends in an accepted Flag or a named Cut;
a model-authored Claim cannot become a Checkpoint, an unmeasured Turn cannot be guessed, and
self-reported impossibility cannot change Order.

## The Run has sixteen untouched Challenges

The full-window Scenario manifest releases exactly sixteen family-disjoint **Discovery holdouts**.
They span at least five open-string Categories; static, isolated HTTP and isolated raw-TCP delivery;
and the Tool-capability coverage needed by the candidate. Before the Run, the Evaluator classifies
their reference-solve complexity, hidden from the Solver, as five routine, five standard, four hard
and two stretch Challenges. The final-90-minute Challenge is one of the sixteen and is independently
verified as solvable under the candidate's available capability surface.

No Challenge used to choose a model, prompt, Tool, topology, Tier, Order weight or other dial may
enter this set. Once a holdout affects a decision it retires permanently to calibration or
regression evidence. All sixteen sealed holdouts remain the Discovery denominator. An accepted Flag
enters the numerator only with complete Evaluator-attested Target evidence and a passing Isolation
receipt; an unqualified, contaminated, unsolved or unattempted holdout remains in the denominator
and never becomes a qualifying solve. Known-answer regressions prove mechanisms, and Execution
holdouts prove adaptation; neither contributes to this solve rate.

The release candidate passes the Run only when all of these hold:

- the same immutable image, scenario, model/prompt/Tool policy and resource profile complete the
  full 5.5-hour window without Intervention;
- at least five of the sixteen holdouts produce Gate-qualified solves, spanning at least three
  Categories;
- the final-90-minute holdout produces a Gate-qualified solve;
- two concurrent Lanes hold independent Leases, at least one Specialist Engagement runs, and the
  planned Boot replacement preserves and fences their state correctly;
- one classified native-route-local fault invokes the independently implemented CPA route without
  duplicate work or effects, after which the Run continues to the same solve floor; and
- the reserved tail completes submission reconciliation, Instance cleanup, evidence flush and
  Promotion with every full-Run safety row passing.

A Run that ends before 5.5 hours is incomplete and fails the Gate. A Run that lasts 5.5 hours but
misses a threshold is a full-window Run and fails the Gate. A rig or Evaluator defect may invalidate
the evidence and require a replacement Run; it remains retained as diagnostic evidence. A valid
Solver failure remains a failed Gate attempt. After a material fix, the changed candidate requires
a new full-window Run. An unchanged candidate is never rerun merely to obtain luck. v2 requires one
passing full-window Run, not one attempt forever and not permission to erase failed attempts.

## Capacity is bounded on the pinned machine

The Gate binds to the pinned Colima allocation of 8 CPUs, 24 GiB RAM and 100 GiB disk. The Solver
may use at most 6 CPUs, 16 GiB RAM, 2,048 PIDs and 40 GiB of writable Run state. The scenario
reserves 2 CPUs, 8 GiB RAM and at least 20 GiB of disk for the rig, trusted control and Recovery.
Touching a reserve, Board throttling caused by the Solver or any hard-envelope breach fails the
applicable row.

Receipts report p50, p95, peak and distributions for wall time, time to first Flag, Flags/hour,
tokens/Flag and every available token class, Board requests/latency, CPU, RAM, PIDs, disk, network,
state/image growth, coordination/idle cost and instrumentation overhead. Timing separates Attempt
admission-to-accepted-verdict from Candidate discovery and receipt sealing, and records per-Turn,
Tool, Board, wait and Recovery spans. Resource sampling is five seconds normally and one second
around injected faults. Cuts remain censored rather than receiving invented completion times. Turn
wall time less observed Tool and controller time is labelled `harness/transport/model residual`; it
is never called reasoning time. Unsupported timing or usage remains `unknown`. With one full Run
these measurements are descriptive, not estimates of competition-wide performance or causal proof
of efficiency.

The Gate reports the exact `solved / 16` denominator and a two-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson interval
for the Challenge-level solve proportion. It reports all three matched-pair deltas plus their
minimum, median and maximum, not a significance claim. Timing and resource distributions retain raw
order statistics. No interval converts one Run, sixteen selected Challenges or three matched pairs
into a claim about official competition performance.

Routing evidence advances through four named stages: deterministic fake, short controlled Run,
three short seeded matched pairs over independent Challenge families, then the one full 5.5-hour
Run. The matched pairs calibrate each optional Triage-Judge, Specialist/topology and model/effort
change while holding every other causal axis fixed. Comparisons decide in this order:
verified Flags, time to first Flag and Flags/hour, then tokens/Flag and resource tails. A policy that
loses on the primary outcomes stays disabled; a tie retains the simpler policy. The mandatory
governance comparison holds the same seed, corpus, releases, faults, model, effort, Tools, route and
safety boundary while comparing full v2 with the lean persistent Solve Lead. Mandatory safety is
never disabled to make the baseline cheaper; regression fails only when full v2 breaches its
absolute Run floor or Resource envelope. These trials do not create another full-window Gate
requirement. Pilot evidence fixes exclusions, stopping rules and lower-level dials before
confirmatory results are read.

The Evaluator also reports route and Tier quality from sealed hidden labels: the Order rank and
admission of each eventually Gate-qualified Challenge, the share of Attempt time spent on those
Challenges, and absolute Base-Tier error. For that metric only, routine, standard, hard and stretch
map to ordinals 1, 2, 3 and 4; error is the absolute difference from the Base Tier at first
acquisition. These labels remain unavailable to the Solver and to pre-Run policy selection. An
optional policy stays disabled if it worsens those measures without improving the earlier primary
outcomes.

The Triage-Judge comparison uses identical sparse, tied, statistically meaningful and changing-Crowd
snapshots across three arms: **deterministic-only**; **always-model**; and **evidence-gated blended**,
which uses deterministic evidence first and invokes one batched judge only for the remaining
ambiguity. Its denominator is every snapshot batch at the declared Intake and release cadence, so
avoiding calls by silently skipping a batch cannot look efficient. The evidence-gated blended arm
remains enabled only if it preserves or improves the primary Flag and speed outcomes while reducing
unnecessary calls against the always-model arm. Receipts count calls per Run, Boot, Intake, release
batch and Challenge;
startup delay; cached, input, output, reasoning and unknown tokens; subscription movement; and
tokens and wall time per verified Flag. Durable judgement replay and interrupted, missing and
superseded-record faults pass before the arm is admitted.

The automatic-training mechanism proves ingestion, time-causal leakage rejection, immutable
Dataset-snapshot reproduction, bounded candidate fitting, promotion/rollback and the prohibition on
within-Run fitting using controlled fixtures. If independent Board/event groups remain insufficient,
the transparent fallback remains Model champion. That is correct ineligibility, not a v2 failure.

## Promotion preserves the proof, not the secrets

Within 24 hours of the release-candidate build, the host captures the official public landing,
guide and confirmed API surfaces. The compatibility query compares status, content type, normalized
schema and headers, and body/asset digests against both the prior lock and landing page so an HTML
catch-all cannot impersonate an API. Unknown official ADK, PoW and Target behaviour remains explicit
v3/Pending rather than guessed into a passing local profile.

The promoted Gate package permanently retains the sealed Scenario manifest, requirement ledger,
signed Gate receipt, exact Solver/rig/Target image digests, SBOM and Tool manifests, compatibility
lock, sanitized Run capsule, Solve and Isolation receipts, deviations, metric outputs and the
versioned analysis code that reproduced the verdict. Required records, expected observations and
actual receipts resolve through content digests; broken hashes or signatures, unresolved deviations,
missing required rows or secret-bearing Promotion fail the Gate.

Promotion fixtures rotate declared credentials between Runs and place every current and retained
historical value in raw bytes, structured keys and values, escaped encodings and malformed structured
content. The scanner must find each through raw and lossless decoded representations without losing
the malformed bytes. Incomplete credential history, a missed fixture, a decoding loss or an
unscannable reachable byte refuses the whole Promotion.

Private raw Observation bodies, vendor rollouts, oracle material and protected captures remain
outside the repository through the scored event plus 30 days, then follow their bounded retention
and reachability policy. Credentials are never retained in either package. Failed, incomplete and
invalid Runs retain their sanitized facts and reason so the passing candidate's denominator and
history cannot be rewritten.

## Consequences

- ADR-0033's stale mandatory unattended real-Board v2 Run is removed. Local proof replaces its v2
  mechanism role; official compatibility and the real-Instance clause remain v3/Pending.
- One full Run is enough to block or pass v2 only because exhaustive controlled proofs precede it.
  It supplies no claim of statistical superiority, population representativeness or official
  compatibility.
- A failure keeps v2 open. The 13 September target may make v2 late, but it never converts a known
  failed clause into v3 work.
- The live roadmap, README Status, Brunner competition record and current forward references are
  reconciled by [Reconcile the v2 Gate after its evidence contract is
  settled](https://github.com/jerome-queck/incypher-ctf/issues/208).

## Revisit when

- the pinned competition machine or runtime allocation changes;
- a pre-Gate pilot shows the sixteen-Challenge composition cannot keep the admitted Lanes working
  or the fixed envelope cannot preserve control and Recovery reserves;
- official evidence makes ADK, PoW, Target or real-Instance compatibility locally testable; or
- the full Run is invalid or fails and its evidence identifies a material change to the candidate.
